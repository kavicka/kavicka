## 📌 About me

I design and build full-stack products end to end: brand, interface, backend, and the integrations that make software actually work in production, not just in a demo (payments, shipping carriers, auth, AI pipelines, whatever the job needs).

Most of what's below I built alone, start to finish. When I work inside a team, such as at [BeCode](https://becode.sk), I own the part that needs one decisive hand: the product design, the client-facing interface, or the pipeline connecting everything together.
